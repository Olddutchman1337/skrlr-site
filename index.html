Skrlr Games

Games that think, remember and adapt.

Every Skrlr game is designed around AI from the start. Characters are driven by agents that perceive the world, keep memory, form intent and act within the rules of play. An AI director tunes difficulty, pacing and encounters to each player.

PERCEIVE

Read the world: game state, nearby characters and player actions.

REMEMBER

Retain history: encounters, alliances, rivalries and promises.

REASON

Form intent: goals and personality drive plans that fit the moment.

ACT

Shape the world: actions resolve through game rules, so emergent play stays tight.

Built for the feed

Instant sessions

Drop in from a feed and play in seconds.

Shareable & remixable

Moments, runs and levels made to be clipped, shared and remixed.

Creator-led

Hooks that let creators and communities steer what gets played.

Persistent characters

AI characters whose personality and memory carry across channels.
