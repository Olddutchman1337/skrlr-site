The platform

Test agents the way real attackers target them.

AI agents now read email, browse the web, touch files and call tools. Each of those is an attack surface. Skrlr simulates those attacks safely and turns the results into repeatable evaluations.

THREAT / 01

Indirect prompt injection

Hidden instructions in web pages, documents and messages that try to redirect what an agent does.

THREAT / 02

Credential & data exfiltration

Attempts to leak secrets or sensitive data, measured with benign canary tokens instead of real data.

THREAT / 03

Agent hijacking

Taking over an agent’s tools and permissions to perform actions the user never asked for.

THREAT / 04

Multi-turn social engineering

Phishing, pretexting and insider-threat scenarios that build pressure over a whole conversation.

01 SIMULATE

Sandboxed environments

Mock inboxes, chat, files and tools, fully isolated from real systems.

02 ATTACK

Automated scenarios

Adversarial and insider-threat scenarios run against the agent under test.

03 OBSERVE

Tripwires & canaries

Canary tokens and tool-call monitors record exactly when an agent crosses a line.

04 EVALUATE

Repeatable results

Scored, reproducible evaluations to compare agents and catch regressions.

A product your team runs itself. Skrlr is software: run evaluations as part of every release, not as a one-off audit.

Research

The research behind the platform.

Our research is practical: every area feeds directly into how the platform tests agents.

01

Agentic red-teaming & evals

Benchmarks and automated attack harnesses for tool-using agents, built on and compatible with open frameworks such as inspect_ai.

02

AI control & sandboxing

Containment, monitoring and tripwires that assume an agent may try to step outside its intended scope.

03

Safety research

Studying how models fail under adversarial pressure, and building tools that detect those failures.

04

Fine-tuning & model work

Small, specialized models for attack generation, monitoring and scoring.

// supporting work: efficient inference, so large evaluation runs stay affordable.

Skrlr Games · testbed

Games that think, remember and adapt.

Skrlr Games builds AI-first, adaptive games and games made for social platforms. Characters are driven by agents that perceive, remember, reason and act within the rules of play. That makes every session a live look at how agents behave with real people.

PERCEIVE

Read the world: game state, nearby characters and player actions.

REMEMBER

Retain history: encounters, alliances, rivalries and promises.

REASON

Form intent: goals and personality drive plans that fit the moment.

ACT

Shape the world: actions resolve through game rules, so emergent play stays tight.
