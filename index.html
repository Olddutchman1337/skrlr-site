AI-first · Agentic game AI

Characters that reason, remember, and act.

We design games from the ground up around AI. Our characters are driven by real AI agents that perceive the world, keep memories, form intentions, and act within the game’s rules, so the world reacts to you instead of replaying a script.

PERCEIVE

Read the world: game state, nearby characters, what you just did.

REMEMBER

Keep a history: past encounters, promises, grudges, and favors.

REASON

Decide: goals and personality shape plans that fit the moment.

ACT

Change the world: actions are checked against game rules, so emergence stays playable.

Adaptive games that tune themselves to each player

An AI director reads how you play and adjusts difficulty, pacing, story beats, and encounters as you go, aiming to keep every session in the sweet spot between challenge and flow, with tension when you’re cruising and relief when you’re stuck.
