Agentic game AI

Characters that reason, remember, and act.

Every Skrlr game is designed around AI from the first line. Our characters are driven by AI agents that perceive the world, retain memory, form intent, and act within the rules of play. The world responds to you, not to a script.

PERCEIVE

Read the world: game state, nearby characters, and every player action.

REMEMBER

Retain history: encounters, alliances, rivalries, and promises kept or broken.

REASON

Form intent: goals and personality drive plans that fit the moment.

ACT

Shape the world: actions resolve through game rules, so emergent play stays tight.

Adaptive by design

An AI director reads how each person plays and continuously tunes difficulty, pacing, story beats, and encounters, holding every session at the edge of challenge and flow.
